Chapter 3: Hydra Heads
Every hydra head is doing something else. Depending on your champions, they are more or less difficult to deal with.The head of decay or provoke head AOE cleanses debuffs and also can single target cleanse and shield the lowest HPhydro head. If the shield is not broken, the hydrohead gets full healed. The head of mischief always attacks the unit withthe most buffs, stealing buffs, turn meter, and also trying to spread those buffs. Also, he redirect all attacks ifhe's not under a hex debuff. The head of blight or poison head puts out the poison cloud. The poison cloud causes all incoming damage to deliver weakheads. So, you can't really deal damage anymore nor debuff the enemies. The head of ref or damage head just deals a lotof damage. Also has a counter that goes up every time you attack him. If it reaches 15, he counterattacks you, dealing big damage. The head of tormentor fearhead true fears you every time you attack him. And the head of suffering or tank head pretty muchsupports the other heads with more resistance, damage mitigation, and all protection.
