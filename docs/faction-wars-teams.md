# Balanced Faction Wars Teams

Based on the September 11, 2026 [champion roster](champions-2026-09-11.md) and your
[faction tier worksheet](./faction-wars.md). These are proposed **Normal Faction Wars progression teams**, aiming for
reliable clears and eventually three stars, not tested stage-21 clears or Hard-mode presets.

## How to Use These Teams

- Each faction gets five distinct owned champion copies. Where you own duplicates, use the developed copy: level-60
  Talenna, Uugo, and Gnarlhorn; level-50 High Khatun, Achak, Runekeeper, Thylessia, Suiren, and Tolog; the fully booked
  level-50 Mordecai; and level-50 Duedan and level-40 Shadowbow Tirlac. Any of your equivalent rank-3/level-11 Warmaiden
  copies can fill her slot.
- These are target compositions after necessary development, not claims that every listed level-1/40/50 champion is
  ready today. Trial supports at 50 before promoting; prioritize a carry and a durable healer/reviver at 60.
- Free gear swaps apply: borrow strong gear between crypt runs. Hydra use does not lock a champion out of Faction Wars.
- A balanced team needs damage, survival, and control, not necessarily one champion in every worksheet role. Decrease
  DEF is valuable; a dedicated Hex or Block Buffs slot is not mandatory in every crypt.
- For three stars, finish with all five champions alive. Revive before the final kill. Boss immunities and stage
  mechanics can make a wave-control slot less useful at the boss; substitutions below are deliberate tradeoffs.
- A Mythical's forms occupy one slot. Gharol/Lazarius cannot supply both forms' ongoing passives simultaneously.

**Priority is within each faction:** P1 = first improvements, P2 = next, P3 = conditional/low-priority spending. Ready =
already level 60, booked, and masteries complete under the agreed rule; trial a suitable loadout before spending more.
Ready does not guarantee a stage clear or confirm ascension. Priorities can tie across different resource types. Level
up any underleveled team member enough to survive before testing, regardless of priority. Next Improvement uses the
September 11 snapshot; skip work you have since completed. Masteries use the agreed `UnUsedT3MasScrolls = 950`
assumption. For level-50 trials, buy no full mastery package automatically; complete masteries when committing to a
six-star build. Check skill ranks before booking: the export only gives totals.

## Investment Order Across Factions

Do not build eighty champions at once. Work on whichever crypt is closest to additional stars and fits the current
opening schedule. These are useful development clusters, not a claim to know your current stage in each crypt.

1. **Overlap with Hydra:** Khamir, Rector, Wythir, Mordecai, and High Khatun. Reuse those investments before funding
   unrelated substitutes. Keep the [Hydra priorities](hydra-teams-2026-09-11.md) as the main resource order.
2. **Existing developed cores:** trial High Elves, Dark Elves, Ogryn Tribes, and Undead Hordes with borrowed gear. Build

## Build and Stage Notes

- For later Normal stages, start around **200-230 SPD on supports/controllers**, **180-210 on damage dealers**, and
  **200-250 ACC on champions whose debuffs must land**. These are practical planning targets, not stage-specific
  guarantees. Use better available gear freely and check actual enemy stats when repeated resists are the issue.
- Aim for roughly **40-60k HP and 2.5-3k DEF on supports** where feasible; prioritize survival over damage on revivers.
  No universal RES target is required. Match any deliberate resistance build to the particular boss/wave.
- Damage dealers generally want **100% CR**, then the correct scaling stat and CD. Pure HP Burn/Poison damage does not
  need crit. Vrask and Dhukk are important crit-dependent utility exceptions; Hellmut's A2 also needs a crit setup.
- Unlike Hydra, many FW waves can be Stunned, Frozen, Slept, Provoked, or have turn meters reduced. Check the boss
  separately: wave-control success does not establish that the boss accepts those effects.
- Stagger control skills rather than using every Stun/Provoke together. Save cooldowns on a nearly cleared wave so the
  next wave cannot immediately act freely. Damage wakes Sleep; plan Kymar/Conellia timing accordingly.
- Watch specific encounters: Valkyrie waves punish careless buffing; buff/debuff swaps can turn protection against you;
  HP-swap bosses may need burst timing or a Destroy-set strategy if fights stall. Do not apply those adjustments blindly
  to every crypt. Record the exact stage and failure before changing the five.
- Choose a leader with an applicable all-battles or FW aura. Arena- or Dungeon-only auras are not FW bonuses.
